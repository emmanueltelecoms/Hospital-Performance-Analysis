# Public Hospital Utilization & Performance Analysis

##  Project Overview
This project was completed as part of a qualifying assessment for an Internship program at **Digital World Tech Academy**. [https://digitalworldedu.com.ng/] After completing my Data Analytics training with the academy, this project served as a "capstone" to demonstrate my ability to clean data, perform deep analysis, and build an interactive dashboard using Excel[cite: 1, 2].

[https://digitalworldedu.com.ng/] The goal of this analysis is to evaluate how public hospitals are performing by looking at patient flow, the quality of care (mortality rates), and how efficiently beds are being used[cite: 3].

##  Tools Used
* **Excel:** Data Cleaning, PivotTables, Advanced Formulas (IFERROR, VLOOKUP), and Conditional Formatting.
* **Data Visualization:** Clustered Bar Charts, Map Charts, and Interactive Slicers.

##  Key Findings & Insights

### 1. Operational Efficiency (Discharge Rate)
**The Big Number:** The system has an overall **90% Discharge Rate**.
* **Insight:** Most hospitals are moving patients through effectively. **National Hospital Abuja** is the leader here with a 94% efficiency rate.
**Action:** Facilities like **Central Hospital Benin (86%)** were identified as areas that need better discharge protocols to free up beds faster.

### 2. Patient Outcomes (Mortality Analysis)
**The Big Number:** The overall mortality rate is **5%**.
**Insight:** Analysis showed that the **Outpatient department** has a higher-than-average mortality rate of **6.8%**.
**Action:** This suggests a need for better emergency triage in outpatient units to catch high-risk patients sooner..

### 3. Service Demand & Geography
**Top Department:** **Pediatrics** is the busiest department in the network.
**Regional Hub:** **Kaduna State** recorded the highest hospital utilization.
**Most Used Services:** **Surgery** and **Delivery** are the top reasons people visit these hospitals.

##  Actionable Recommendations
Based on the data findings, I recommend the following strategic steps:

 **Audit Outpatient Triage:** Investigate the high mortality rate in the Outpatient department. [cite_start]Implementing a prioritization system can help catch critical cases early. 
**Support Underperforming Facilities:** Provide operational support to hospitals with lower discharge rates, like **Central Hospital Benin**, to improve patient flow. 
**Optimize Bed Turnover:** Review procedures at hospitals with high **Average Length of Stay** to ensure beds are being freed up efficiently for new patients. 
**Regional Resource Allocation:** Direct additional medical supplies and staff to **Kaduna State** to match the high geographic demand identified in the analysis. 
## Length of Stay (Bed Occupancy)
On average, patients stay for about **2.95 days**
**Asaba General Hospital** has the longest stays (over 3.2 days), which indicates their beds stay full longer than other hospitals.

## Conclusion
This project proves that data can help hospital managers make better decisions. By looking at these charts, a manager can see exactly which hospital needs more staff, which department needs better equipment, and where patients are waiting too long.
